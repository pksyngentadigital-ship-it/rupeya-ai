🤖 Rupeya's analysis

Updated today · 7:18 PM

The thesis. Engro is one of a small handful of Pakistani holding companies that has compounded book value at >15% per year through multiple political and currency cycles. Its fertilizer subsidiary (EFERT, separately listed) prints cash through inflation, foods owns >50% of dairy, and telecom infrastructure (towers) earns USD-linked rents — three uncorrelated streams, all Shariah-compliant under KMI screens.

What's working. Q1 FY26 net income +18% YoY on better fertilizer realizations and a stable PKR. Free cash flow is 94% of net income — earnings are real, not accruals. Net debt to equity sits at 0.6x, well within long-term tolerance. Dividend payout is sustainable at 50% with FCF coverage of 1.9x.

What to watch. A re-imposition of gas subsidy reform delays would compress fertilizer margins. Sub-conglomerate discount could persist if management does not list Engro Foods by FY27. We'd flip from STRONG BUY to BUY if the price re-rates above PKR 360 without commensurate earnings growth.
