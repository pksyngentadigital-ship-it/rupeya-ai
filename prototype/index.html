Common questions

Is Rupeya.ai a SECP-licensed advisor?

No. Rupeya.ai is an educational research tool. We surface AI-generated analysis of public PSX filings and news. We don't tell you what to do with your money — we help you think clearly about it.

Will the AI hallucinate financial numbers?

No. Every number you see (intrinsic value, ROE, score) is computed by deterministic Python code from filings. The AI only translates numbers into plain-English narrative.

What stocks are covered?

The ~80 most-traded PSX-listed companies at launch, expanding to the full KSE-100 and beyond.

Do I need a broker account?

Yes — you'll execute trades through your existing broker. Rupeya.ai is research, not a brokerage. We don't touch your money.
